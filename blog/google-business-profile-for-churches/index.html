Google Business Profile for Churches: A Local SEO Checklist

A step-by-step local listing checklist for churches that want neighbors to find them.

November 23, 2026By Modernized Ministry4 min read

A step-by-step local listing checklist for churches that want neighbors to find them.

Why your church's map listing matters

Someone searching for a church nearby may encounter your Google listing before your homepage. If the hours, location, or website link are wrong, the first impression becomes confusing or frustrating. A well-maintained profile makes information easier to find.

The listing should reflect a real, eligible organization at its real location. Do not create duplicate or fictitious listings to target nearby towns.

Claim and verify the correct profile

Find your church's existing profile and follow Google's current ownership and verification process. Use the exact public church name, real address when eligible, and the best category that accurately describes the congregation.

Keep account recovery options current and restrict manager access to trusted staff. Do not let a departing volunteer become the only person who can edit your listing.

Check your essential information

Confirm the map pin, phone number, website URL, service times, and any office hours your listing displays. Update holiday and special event hours where applicable. If a service moves locations, edit the website and listing together.

Add accessible photos of the exterior, entrance, parking, and gathering area, with appropriate permissions. Make directions actionable for someone arriving for the first time.

Use reviews and posts responsibly

People may use public reviews to understand a church's hospitality. Invite honest feedback without rewards, pressure or scripted language. When responding, avoid revealing someone's personal religious involvement or sensitive information.

Share relevant public updates where the platform allows, but remember that your own church website should remain the authoritative source for event details.

Connect the profile to a stronger website

The Google listing and your website should agree on basic facts. The homepage can introduce the church; linked pages can explain beliefs, children, service formats, and Plan Your Visit.

Review Google's own guidelines for representing your business and our church SEO guide before considering paid promotions.

Frequently asked questions

Can a church have a Google Business Profile?

Eligible churches with a qualifying real-world presence can manage their business information; follow Google's current eligibility and verification policies.

Will adding keywords to the church name improve Maps visibility?

Use the church's genuine public name. Adding promotional keywords that are not part of the name can violate platform policies.
