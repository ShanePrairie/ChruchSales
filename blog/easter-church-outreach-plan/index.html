Easter Church Outreach Plan: Six Weeks to Welcome New Families

A six-week Easter invitation plan that keeps hospitality ahead of hype.

November 23, 2026By Modernized Ministry4 min read

A six-week Easter invitation plan that keeps hospitality ahead of hype.

Six weeks out: Set the invitation

Decide what Easter services will actually offer: accurate times, locations, childcare, parking, accessibility, and whether an RSVP is useful or required. Build a single Easter page on your church site with these details.

State the invitation simply. 'Join us for Easter worship at 9 or 11 a.m. Everyone is welcome' is clearer than insider phrases that assume people know the format.

Five weeks out: Update local discovery

Check the homepage banner, Google Business Profile, event calendar, and social profiles. Make the Easter landing page mobile-friendly, fast, and easy to share. Use descriptive event headings including the church's real city or neighborhood.

Avoid creating duplicate doorway pages for towns you do not serve. The useful local details belong on the real service page.

Four weeks out: Prepare your people

Ask members to invite friends through genuine personal conversations. Give volunteers a short invitation card or URL, not a rigid script. Train the hospitality team to guide people from parking to children's check-in without creating confusion.

Plan how newcomers can ask questions in person or online before the holiday.

Three to two weeks out: Share helpful content

Publish a welcome video, a short explanation of the service format, and a reminder about arrival time. Promote a community service opportunity if one exists independently of the holiday campaign.

If paid ads fit your budget, keep a defined geographic audience, modest spend cap, and a clear link to accurate service details.

Final week and Easter Sunday: Deliver the promise

Test the service page, directions, contact form, and livestream link. Confirm accessibility details, overflow parking, and childcare staffing. Brief greeters and ensure first-time guests can participate without being singled out.

Have an optional connection card, but never require a guest to submit personal information to attend worship.

Week after: Care for new relationships

Thank people who requested follow-up, share sermon links if useful, and offer one clear next step. Invite honest feedback from volunteers and guests. Keep your Easter page accurate after the service rather than leaving expired event information prominent.

Build on our welcome plan and 90-day outreach strategy for connection beyond one holiday.

Frequently asked questions

When should a church start Easter outreach?

Six weeks gives many congregations enough time to coordinate details, volunteers, local information and invitations without overloading the team.

What matters most on an Easter church landing page?

Correct service times and location, first-visit expectations, family details, and a simple contact or visit action.
