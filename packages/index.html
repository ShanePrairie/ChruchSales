COMMON QUESTIONS

The questions
you should ask.

Who owns the domain and content?

Your church should retain control over its domain and its own content. Ownership, hosting and handoff details are put in writing before a project starts.

Can we start with the website and add texts later?

Absolutely. Begin with the essentials and add opt-in welcome messages, campaigns, or ministry tools as your team is ready.

Are texts and emails unlimited?

Message volume and provider charges depend on the chosen platform and plan. We explain those costs before setup and make clear which services are included in monthly care.

How long does a website take?

Timing depends on scope, content readiness, approvals, and integrations. You'll get a project schedule after discovery rather than an unrealistic blanket promise.

Can we see an actual church website?

Yes. Explore the fully interactive Haven Church demonstration. It's a fictional concept built to showcase our flagship design approach—not a claim about an existing client.
