PRIVACY · VERSION 1

Your information
matters.

We only ask for basic contact information and details you choose to send when requesting a consultation.

When configured, the inquiry form forwards your name, email, church name, selected interest, and message to the business email address through a third-party email delivery provider. We use this information to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell contact details or enroll you in marketing messages merely for asking a question.

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first measurement tool that does not use cookies, local storage, or fingerprinting, to count page views and measure page performance in aggregate. We do not use advertising pixels or sell visitor data. Operational hosting logs may be processed by the hosting provider. Messages you send are handled subject to the email provider's data practices.

The Haven Church flagship and outreach journey are fictional demonstrations; entries into the sample visit-planning form are not saved or transmitted. For access, correction, or deletion requests, contact hello@modernizedministry.com.
