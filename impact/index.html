01 / REAL RESEARCH

The opportunity
is measurable.

These are national survey findings. They show how people engage with faith communities; they do not measure results produced by Modernized Ministry.

CHURCH DISCOVERY37%

Used church websites while searching.

Among Protestant churchgoers who switched congregations, 37% used church websites to evaluate options; 29% used social media and 27% online search tools. Respondents could select multiple resources (n=791).

Lifeway Research — 2023 report ↗
HYBRID CONNECTION26%

Worshipped online or in a hybrid pattern.

In a 2024–2025 survey of attenders, 26% participated predominantly virtually or alternated between online and in-person worship.

Hartford Institute — 2025 findings ↗
THE DIGITAL SHIFT75%

Offered online worship.

A 2024 congregational technology study reported 75% offering virtual worship, versus 45% before the pandemic. That is a shift in availability, not a guaranteed attendance gain.

Hartford Institute — 2024 study ↗
GIVING IN A DIGITAL AGE30%

Of gifts arrived through online giving.

Across surveyed congregations in 2023, about 30% of donation value came through online giving, versus 10% in 2020. This measures a change in giving channel, not revenue growth caused by technology.

Hartford Institute — 2024 financial report ↗
WHAT RESEARCH SAYS ABOUT FINANCIAL HEALTH$2,428 vs. $1,818

Median annual giving income per attendee

In the same 2024 Hartford report, congregations using online giving “a lot” reported a median $2,428 per capita income; those not offering online giving reported $1,818.

Important: this is an observational association—not proof that adding online giving or hiring our company will produce that difference.

03 / WHERE IMPACT COMES FROM

From first click
to church family.

01

Be discoverable.

Make location, service times, beliefs, ministries, and a visit plan effortless to find on any phone.

02

Lower the nerves.

Show what to expect, where to park, and how families are welcomed before guests arrive.

03

Follow up with care.

Send permission-based messages after a visit, offer a next step, and give people a way to opt out.

04

Measure what matters.

Track inquiries, planned visits, actual visits, return visits, and voluntary connections—not vanity metrics.

The truth about growth

Strong digital outreach can remove barriers. It does not replace hospitality, faith, compelling ministry, or meaningful relationships. Our job is to help the right people find the right next step.
